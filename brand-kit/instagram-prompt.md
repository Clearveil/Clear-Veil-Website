## Design system (match exactly)
Canvas
- Feed posts and carousel slides: 1080 × 1350 px (4:5). Stories and Reels covers: 1080 × 1920 px.
- Pure black background (#000). Add depth with one or two very soft white glows: a large radial gradient of white at 6–10% opacity fading to transparent, usually near the top. Never flat grey, never any colour.
- Generous margins: at least 88 px on every side. Lots of empty black space; let the type lead.

Colour: black and white only
- Main text #FFFFFF. Supporting text white at 62% opacity. Small labels white at 52%.
- Hairlines and dividers: white at 8–14% opacity.
- Photos (if any) are always black and white (grayscale, slightly higher contrast).

Typography: DM Sans (Google Fonts)
- Headlines: weight 400 (regular, never bold), letter-spacing -0.045em, line-height 1.02, balanced line breaks. Big: about 110–150 px for the main headline on a 1080-wide post.
- Headline treatment: white fading to translucent, `linear-gradient(100deg, #fff 30%, rgba(255,255,255,0.46) 120%)` clipped to the text, so the end of each line softly fades.
- Body: weight 400, 34–40 px, white at 62%, line-height 1.5, max about 22 words per line.
- Big stat numbers: weight 400, tight tracking (-0.05em), with a vertical white-to-60% gradient.
- The wordmark "CLEAR VEIL" (the attached SVG) is the only mono-style element. Never retype it in another font.

Liquid glass (the core material; use for cards, pills, buttons, stat panels)
- Fill: `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.065) 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0.012) 60%, rgba(255,255,255,0.045) 100%)`
- Rim: a 1–2 px border brighter at the top-left: `linear-gradient(165deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.3), rgba(255,255,255,0.1) 28%, rgba(255,255,255,0.03) 55%, rgba(255,255,255,0.07))`
- Light inside the glass: `inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.22), inset 0 12px 24px -16px rgba(255,255,255,0.28), inset 0 -10px 22px -16px rgba(255,255,255,0.14)`
- Soft shadow below: `0 18px 40px -18px rgba(0,0,0,0.85)`
- Corners: cards 48–64 px radius at post scale; pills and buttons fully rounded.

Recurring elements
- Eyebrow pill: a small glass capsule above a headline with a glowing white dot (a 12 px white circle with a soft white glow) followed by a short label like "Case study", "Paid ads" or "How it works", in white at 78%, about 26 px.
- Button: a clear glass capsule, white text weight 500, e.g. "Book a 15-minute call". No arrows on buttons.
- Tags: small rounded pills with a hairline border, white at 62%, e.g. "Google Ads", "Website", "SEO".
- Stat panel: a glass card with a big number and a small label under it; several stats sit in one glass band split by hairlines.
- Footer of every post: the mark plus the CLEAR VEIL wordmark, small (wordmark about 180 px wide), at the bottom left or centred, white at about 85%.
